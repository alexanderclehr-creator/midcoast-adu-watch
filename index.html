How to use this page
  1. Use the tabs at the top: Best options, Map, All listings, Towns & rents, and Changes & method. The filters on the left apply to every tab.
  2. Filter on the left: price, house size, land and distance to water take real numbers. Leave a box blank for no limit.
  3. Sort and stack filters. Pick a sort order (score, yield, payback, price and more). Feature chips such as In-law, Barn and Public sewer all must match, so each one you add narrows the list.
  4. Set the ADU assumptions: the ADU size in sq ft and the build cost per sq ft. The build cost is size × cost per sq ft. Each listing shows the monthly rent for the house plus the ADU, and the all-in cost (price plus ADU).
  5. Read the Best options, then the Map and All listings tabs. On the map, click a town to filter to its area. Switch All listings between a list and tiles. Click a table heading to sort. Click any card or row for photos, water distances, town rules, rents and the Redfin link.
  6. Star the houses you like, then pick the “Starred” feature chip. Stars, sort and filters stay in your own browser.
  7. Check Changes after each update for new listings, price changes and listings that went off the market.

How it works

  • Listings: active single-family and manufactured homes on Redfin in the midcoast counties (Sagadahoc, Lincoln, Knox, Waldo), Brunswick, Harpswell and the Blue Hill peninsula. Lot size, sewer, water, zoning and price history come from each listing. Each update adds a snapshot, so new listings, price changes and listings that went off the market show up under Changes.
  • Jurisdiction: 1 to 5 for building a rental ADU on a house lot. 5 means by right, detached allowed, no size cap and a code officer's permit only. Island towns are capped at 2.5 for freight and labour cost.
  • Rents:
  • Vacancy: ACS 2020–2024 rental vacancy for each town (units for rent ÷ all rental units), blended with the county rate as if the county added 150 rental units, then floored at 5%. Small towns have wide margins of error, so the blend pulls them toward the county.
  • Cash yield: 12 months of rent after vacancy, less 12 monthly payments, divided by the cash put in (the down payment). “House only” uses the price; “With ADU” adds the ADU build cost to the price and finances both together. Closing costs, upkeep, utilities and management are not deducted. The score still uses rent yield before financing (rent after vacancy ÷ all-in cost).
  • Payback period: the years until monthly cash flow (rent after vacancy less the payment) adds up to the cash put in. Rent grows each year at the rent growth rate; the payment stays fixed. Early negative years count against payback, so a house that loses money at first needs those losses repaid too. When cash flow starts negative, the page also shows the year it turns positive and the yearly shortfall until then. You can turn payback off.
  • Monthly payment: principal and interest on a 30-year fixed loan, plus property tax and home insurance, the same inputs as Redfin’s payment calculator. The default rate is Redfin’s Bankrate 30-year rate for Maine on the update day; you can change it and the down payment. Tax is the listing’s annual tax ÷ price, as Redfin does (the town median when a listing has no usable tax). Insurance is Redfin’s estimate for the house; where Redfin’s figure is not yet stored, it is fitted to Redfin’s estimates for other listings (about 0.2% of price a year). Below 20% down, mortgage insurance of 0.75% of the loan a year is added. HOA dues are not included. “House rent covers payment” compares the main house rent alone (no ADU, no vacancy) with the payment on the price. “Rent with ADU covers payment” compares house plus ADU rent with the payment on price plus ADU build cost.
  • Rent growth:
  • Score: the weighted average of five 0–1 scores: jurisdiction, low price (log scale, $150,000 to $2,000,000), house size (800 to 2,800 sf), land (log scale up to 5 acres) and rent yield (3% to 12%). An existing in-law apartment adds 3 points and a barn 1. A Rockport lot on town sewer loses 10 points, because the town has no sewer treatment capacity.
  • Land: state law requires no extra lot area for one ADU. In practice a septic lot needs room for a larger or second system, so 1 acre or more is a safer screen. Shore lots in several Blue Hill peninsula towns must meet full dimensions for each added unit.
  • Water: straight-line distance from each listing’s map point to the ocean shoreline (Census TIGER 2024 coastline, bays and coves) and to the nearest lake, pond or river of 5 acres or more. Tidal rivers such as the St. George count as rivers. It is not road or path distance and does not mean the lot has frontage or rights of access.
  • Sharing: everything here is built into the page, so people without a Claude account can open it from a public link. Stars, weights and filters stay in each viewer’s browser.

Planning reference only. Confirm the town’s current ADU ordinance, septic capacity and shoreland status before an offer. Not legal or financial advice.
